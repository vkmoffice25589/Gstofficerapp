Taxpayer Register Upload Guidelines
State & Centre Combined Taxpayer List

      
    

    

      
    
        
  • Download the MIS_REG_03 "List of Active/Cancelled/Suspended Taxpayer" reports from the GST portal, unmodified
    • 
        
  • Upload each list into its matching zone — Active, Cancelled or Suspended
    • 
        
  • Registration status is auto-detected from each file's own title, so files can't be mixed up between zones
    • 
        
  • Re-uploading a list for the same status replaces that data — GSTINs are matched and updated, not duplicated
    • 
        
  • Taxpayer Register data drives the Active/Cancelled/Suspended collectibility classification used in Reports and Notices
    •
